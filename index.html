About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Shankar Sastry.

Before UC Berkeley, I worked as a Research Scientist at Lockheed Martin for y years, the I taught engineering at Cañada College. At Lockheed Martin, I performed aerothermal analysis and design for hypersonic vehicles earlier in my career, and towards the end, I conducted AI and robotics research. I hold a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Southern California and an M.S. in Aeronautics and Astronautics from Purdue.

Research interests

My research develops algorithmic frameworks for multi-agent cyber-physical systems operating under uncertainty in complex, dynamic environments. I combine learning-based methods with control theory, optimization, game theory, and mechanism design. I am interested in the safety and alignment of multi-agent intelligent systems and their interactions, for problems requiring efficient, sustained coordination and incentive design among self-interested agents. Applications of my work include next-generation air mobility and multi-robot systems for disaster response.

I co-organize the Berkeley–Hopkins Seminar Series on Learning-Enabled Multi-Agent Systems (LEMAS) and its weekly study group.
